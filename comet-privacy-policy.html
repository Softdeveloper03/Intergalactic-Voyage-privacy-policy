About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Comet, a mobile game published on Google Play under the package name com.intergalactic.app ("we", "us", "our" refer to the developer of Comet). It explains what information the game handles and the choices you have.

Information the game stores

To keep your progress between sessions, Comet saves a small amount of game data locally on your device:

  • Your best score and deepest Ascension reached
  • Game settings, such as sound, music and vibration preferences
  • Whether you've completed the first-run introduction

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to your identity.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, microphone audio or any other personal information.

Device permissions

PermissionWhy the game uses it
VibrationTo give light haptic feedback when you release and catch the comet. You can turn this off in the game's settings.

Comet does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, files or phone calls.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with anyone, because we don't collect it in the first place.

Deleting your data

All game data is stored on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing Comet's app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game. Once removed, it cannot be recovered.

Children

Comet is suitable for a general audience. Because the game does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Union and the United Kingdom, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Since Comet does not collect personal data on our servers, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you are always welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how the game handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before those features are released.
