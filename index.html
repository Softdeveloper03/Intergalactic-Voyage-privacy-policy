Information stored on your device

To keep your progress between sessions, Voyager Comet saves a small amount of game data locally on your device:

  • Your best score and deepest Ascension reached
  • Game settings, such as sound, music and vibration preferences
  • Whether you've completed the first-run introduction

This game data stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to your identity.

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone audio or any other information that directly identifies you.

Analytics (Google Firebase)

Voyager Comet uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google LLC, to understand how players use the game so we can fix problems and improve gameplay. When you play with an internet connection, Firebase Analytics may automatically collect:

  • App interactions: events such as game sessions started, levels or Ascensions reached, screens viewed and session length
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language and app version
  • Identifiers: a Firebase app instance ID and, where available, your device's Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location: country or region, derived from your IP address (not precise GPS location)
  • Diagnostics: basic information such as first-open time and app updates

This information is used only for analytics: measuring engagement, understanding which features are used, and improving the game's stability and design. It is not used to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with any information that directly identifies you.

Analytics data is retained according to our Firebase retention settings and is then automatically deleted. Google processes this data under its own terms; see Privacy and Security in Firebase and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can limit the use of your advertising ID at any time by resetting it or deleting it in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. The analytics data described above is shared only with Google (Firebase), which acts as our service provider to process it on our behalf. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the App.

Deleting your data

Game progress and settings are stored on your device. You can delete them at any time by clearing Voyager Comet's app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game. Once removed, they cannot be recovered.

Analytics data is automatically deleted at the end of its retention period. You can also ask us to delete analytics data associated with your installation by emailing us at the address below; we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Voyager Comet is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
